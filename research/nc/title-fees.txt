Please do one of the following: Reload Page View Site Map Email NCDOT Page Content ​​​​​​Title and registration fees can be paid through a variety of common payment methods . The N.C. Division of Motor Vehicles uses an online service, called PayIt, that allows you to take advantage of completing multiple services in one secure transaction. PayIt collects a $3 fee per online transaction​ plus​ a 1.85 percent card processing fee ​, These fees are used ​to deliver quality services more efficiently with no upfront costs to NCDMV. The State of North Carolina does not collect or benefit from the transaction fee. ​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ Title fees​​ ​​Registration fees Re​gistration fees vary depending on the type of vehicle being plated. ​​ Regional fees and taxes Notary fees The following are maximum fees that may be charged.
