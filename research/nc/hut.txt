Please do one of the following: Reload Page View Site Map Email NCDOT Page Content ​​​​​North Carolina generally collects what's known as the highway-use tax – instead of sales tax – on vehicles whenever a title is transferred. Vehicles are also subject to property taxes , which the N.C. Division of Motor Vehicles collects, as defined by law, on behalf of counties. Revenue from the highway-use tax goes to the North Carolina Highway Trust Fund and the North Carolina's General Fund and is earmarked for road improvements.
