Disability Access Parking

Persons with temporary disabilities may be issued a temporary placard valid for a period not to exceed six months.
Persons with permanent disabilities who are owners of private passenger automobiles to include pickup trucks and recreational vehicles with a gross vehicle weight of 12,000 pounds or less may be issued one blue disability access decal (per vehicle) to be adhered to the top left corner of the license plate and one windshield placard (per person), or two windshield placards.

32-6-230, 32-6-231, and 40-12-300

Registration Fees:
Other Information:
Legislative Act 2024-346 replaces the current international disability access symbol with a new dynamic disability access symbol. As a result of the act, the symbols on both placards and decals will change. The act is effective January 1, 2026.
Dynamic International Symbol of Disability Access decal/placard (Effective January 2026)
Effective January 2026, newly issued temporary or permanent disability placards/decals will reflect the new dynamic symbol.
Registrants who currently have disability placards/decals with the previous symbol will keep the old design until their renewal month in 2027 (the expiration date reflected on the current placards).
Therefore, from 2026 until the end of 2027, there will be vehicles on the road with both the old and new symbols.

Educator/Retired Educator
Issued To:
Active – All residents of Alabama (or their spouses) who are currently employed by a public or private education school system, college, university, institution, or who are active and contributing members of an education retirement pension program, whether it is an in-state or out-of-state program may receive a distinctive Educator license plate to be displayed upon private passenger automobiles, pickup trucks, motorcycles, pleasure motor vehicles (i.e., recreational vehicles).
Retired – All residents of Alabama (or their spouses) who are receiving monthly retirement checks from an education retirement pension program from any state may receive a distinctive Educator license plate to be displayed upon private passenger automobiles, pickup trucks, motorcycles, pleasure motor vehicles (i.e., recreational vehicles).
Code Section:
32-6-302
Required Documents
Active – The registrant is required to present documentation or certification to the license plate issuing official that the registrant (or their spouse) is currently employed in public or private education as provided by law. Documentation must be presented annually in order to substantiate that the registrant is eligible to obtain these license plates.
Retired – The registrant is required to present documentation to the license plate issuing official that he/she (or their spouse) is receiving a monthly allotment as a retired education employee. Once a retired educator presents proof that he or she is retired, the proof shall be valid from year to year and the retired educator is not required to resubmit proof of retirement as long as the retired educator does not move to another county. If the retired educator moves to another county, proof of retirement shall be presented upon initial registration in the new county and shall be valid from year to year thereafter.

32-6-270, 32-6-272 and 40-12-244
Required Documents
Proof of being an active or retired firefighter of a paid, part-paid or volunteer fire department or a widow or widower of firefighter who died in the line of duty is required.
The Alabama Forestry Commission will provide a list of eligible volunteer firefighters and the Firefighters’ Personnel Standards and Education Commission will provide a list of all members of paid or part-paid fire departments on their websites for the licensing officials’ offices. The registrant’s name must appear on one of these lists in order to be eligible to receive the Firefighter license plate. Firefighter Tag Certificate for Widow/er Killed in the Line of Duty

A volunteer firefighter who is a resident of Alabama is entitled to a registration fee exemption for one vehicle as provided for in Code Section 40-12-244.
