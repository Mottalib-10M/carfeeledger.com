      Background:

      Fuel Taxes.

      State fuel taxes apply to fuel licensees for the sales of both motor vehicle fuel (includes
      gasoline) and special fuel (includes diesel).  The aggregate rate comprises individual taxes
      enacted at different times and with specific revenue distributions made to certain state
      accounts, and to counties and cities following specified percentage allocations.  Prior to July
      1, 2025, the aggregate tax rate was 49.4 cents per gallon with respect to both motor vehicle
      fuel and special fuel.

      Beginning July 1, 2025, the fuel tax rate for motor vehicle fuel and special fuel was
      increased by 6 cents per gallon, from 49.4 cents to 55.4 cents per gallon.  On July1,
      2025, the fuel tax rate for special fuel was increased by 3 cents per gallon, and will be
      increased to a total of 58.4 cents per gallon by an additional 3 cents per gallon to an
      additional of 61.4 cents per gallon beginning July 1, 2027.  Revenue generated from these
      fuel tax rate increases is distributed to the Motor Vehicle Fund (MVF), except that 2.5
      percent is distributed to incorporated cities, and 2.5 percent is distributed to counties.

      Fuel tax rates, except for the special fuel differential rates, are increased by an inflation
      adjustment factor of 2 percent each year beginning July 1, 2026.  The special fuel
      differential rate is increased by an inflation adjustment factor of 2 percent each year
      beginning July 1, 2028.

      General Retail Sales and Use Taxes.

      Retail sales taxes are imposed on retail sales of most articles of tangible personal property,
      digital products, and some services.  A retail sale is a sale to the final consumer or end user
      of the property, digital product, or service.  If retail sales taxes are not collected when the
      user acquires property, digital products, or services, then use tax applies to the value of the
      property, digital product, or service when used in this state.  The state, all counties, and all
      cities levy retail sales and use taxes.  The state sales and use tax rate is 6.5 percent.  State
      sales and use tax revenues are deposited into the State General Fund.

      The general retail sales tax provides for a deduction from the selling price, and the use tax
      from the value of the article used, for the value of any trade-in property of like kind, for
      certain categories of property, including vehicles, boats, and airplanes.  The trade-in must
      be used as consideration for the purchase or acquisition of the property and must be
      identified clearly on any sales agreement or invoice.  The tax then applies to the remainder
      after the value is deducted.

      Retail Sales and Use Taxes on Acquisitions of Luxury Vehicles, Luxury Aircraft, and
      Recreational Vessels.

      In 2025 legislation was enacted establishing new sales and use taxes on the purchase or
      acquisition of certain luxury vehicles, luxury aircraft, and recreational vessels.

      Beginning January 1, 2026, an 8 percent tax is imposed on the retail sale or use of a non-
      commercial motor vehicle, including motor homes, that exceeds $100,000 in price.  The tax
      applies to the portion of the price or value of the vehicle that exceeds a deduction amount of
      $100,000 in fiscal year 2026.  At the start of each fiscal year, the deduction amount is
      increased by 2 percent.

      Beginning April 1, 2026, an additional 10 percent luxury aircraft tax applies to the sale or
      use of a non-commercial aircraft exceeding $500,000.  The new luxury tax applies only to
      the amount of the sale or the value of the aircraft that exceeds $500,000, treating the
      $500,000 as a deduction.

      Beginning July 1, 2026, a 0.5 percent tax applies to the sale or use of a recreational vessel.

      For each of the new sales taxes enacted in 2025, the value of a trade-in vehicle may not be
      deducted from the vehicle price in determining the tax.  A trade-in may be deducted from
      the value when determining the use tax.

      Aircraft Fuel Tax.

      The aircraft fuel tax is 18 cents per gallon with the revenue deposited into the Aeronautics

      Aircraft Registration Fee.

      Aircraft, including commercial unpiloted aircraft systems, must be registered with the
      Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) in January of each calendar year
      in which the aircraft is to be operated or based in Washington.  The state registration fee is
      $15, with the revenue deposited into the Aeronautics Account.

      Aircraft Excise Tax.

      An annual excise tax is levied for using an aircraft in the state.  The tax ranges from $20 for
      a home built plane to $4,000 for a larger aircraft operated by a commuter air carrier.
      Revenue from the aircraft excise tax is deposited in the Aeronautics Account.  Aircraft
      exempt from the annual excise tax include:
          •   aircraft engaged in interstate commerce;
          •   governmental aircraft;
          •   aircraft registered by a foreign country;
          •   aircraft registered in another state, unless based in this state for 90 days or longer;
          •   aircraft owned by a manufacturer or dealer if part of their stock in trade;
          •   aircraft owned by a manufacturer and used for testing or training purposes; and
          •   commercial unpiloted aircraft systems.

      Tire Fee.

      A $5 per tire fee is imposed on the retail sale of new replacement vehicle tires.  Retailers are
      permitted to retain a portion of the fee for costs associated with the proper management of
      the waste vehicle tires by the retailer.  Of the proceeds to the state, the first $600,000 each
      fiscal year is deposited into the Waste Tire Removal Account, and all remaining amounts
      are deposited into the MVF.

      Payments to Registered Tow Truck Operators for the Release of Vehicles to Indigent
      Persons.

      Registered tow truck operators (RTTOs) who impound vehicles from private or public
      property, or who tow vehicles for law enforcement agencies, are regulated by the
      Department of Licensing (DOL).  The RTTOs may impound, transport, and store
      unauthorized vehicles and may dispose of abandoned vehicles.  An RTTO who has a valid

      and signed impoundment authorization is determined to have a lien upon the impounded
      vehicle for services provided in the towing and storage of the vehicle, unless the
      impoundment is determined to have been invalid.

      A vehicle may be redeemed by the legal owner or owners of the vehicle before the start of
      an abandoned vehicle auction upon payment of towing and storage charges.  If a vehicle
      remains unclaimed, the RTTO must conduct a sale of the vehicle at public auction.  The
      RTTO may not hold a vehicle longer than 90 days without holding an auction for the
      vehicle, except where a law enforcement or judicial order requires it.

      The DOL is required to create a program that was to take effect February 1, 2026, to
      compensate RTTOs for the cost of towing, storage, and other services incurred by RTTOs
      for the towing of vehicles of indigent persons in certain circumstances.  As part of the
      program, the DOL must provide a form to RTTOs on which an individual seeking the
      release of the vehicle can self-certify that the individual is eligible under the program, and
      the RTTO can self-certify that the impound is eligible under the program.  Once the form is
      completed by the RTTO, the RTTO is required to release the vehicle to the applicant and no
      longer has a lien or deficiency claim on the vehicle.

      Following submission of the form by the RTTO and subject to the availability of funds, the
      DOL is required to disburse funds to the eligible RTTO equal to the cost of the tow, storage,
      or other services incurred by the RTTO during the course of the impound.

      Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle Report.

      The Department of Revenue (DOR) must provide to the transportation committees of the
      Legislature a semiannual report on the clean alternative fuel vehicle sales and use tax
      exemption. The report must include information on the number of vehicles that qualified for
      the exemption, the make and model of these vehicles, the dollar amount of exempted state
      retail sales and use taxes, and the estimated future costs of leased vehicles that qualified for
      the exemption.  The report is required August 1, 2028The exemption qualification period
      ended on July 31, 2025.

      Bus and Bus Facilities Grant Program.

      In 2022 the Legislature enacted the Bus and Bus Facilities Brant Program at the WSDOT.
      The purpose of the competitive grant program is to provide transit agencies grants for:
          •  the replacement, expansion, rehabilitation, and purchase of transit rolling stock;
          •  the construction, modification, or rehabilitation of transit facilities; and
          •  funding to adapt to technological change or innovation through the retrofitting of
             transit rolling stock and facilities.

      Driver's Licenses and Identicards.

      The DOL issues driver's licenses and identicards that are valid for up to eight years.  The
      fee for an eight-year driver's license or identicard is $80, and the feefor a six-year driver's
      license or identicard is $60.  Beginning July 1, 2028, and every three years thereafter, the
      per-year fee amounts are increased by $1.  An additional $1flat fee is imposed on each
      issuance or renewal of a driver's license or identicard.

      An at-cost identicard, with the fee amount determined based on the production costs of the
      identicard, may be provided if the applicant is:
           •  is under the age of 25 and does not have a permanent residence address;
           •  receives public assistance; or
           •  is going to be, or has recently been, discharged from certain state and local facilities.

      An enhanced driver license (EDL) or enhanced identicard (EID) may be issued to a
      Washington resident who isa United States citizen if they have confirmed their identity and
      citizenship.  A person applying for an EDL must be at least 18 years old and must pay a fee
      in addition to the regular driver's license or identicard fee.  The additional fee for an eight-
      year EDL or EID is $56, and the fee  for a six-year EDL or EID is $42.

      Toll Collection Communication.

      A notice of civil penalty may be issued by WSDOT when a toll is assessed through use of a
      photo toll system and the toll is not paid by the toll payment due date.  An individual may
      appeal a civil penalty by presenting evidence that a toll bill was not received due to it being
      sent to an incorrect address that has since been corrected.  The envelope containing a toll
      charge bill or a notice of civil penalty must prominently indicate that the contents are time
      sensitive and related to a toll violation.

      Public Transportation Benefit Area Annexations.

      Public Transportation Benefit Areas (PTBA) are local governments established to operate
      public transportation services.  In 2025 the Legislature enacted an optional PTBA
      annexation process that authorizes an annexation of an adjacent city operating a transit
      system and of certain county areas.

      Ferry Credit Card Surcharge.

      In 2025 the Washington State Ferries (WSF) was directed to implement a cost recovery
      surcharge to recover at least 3 percent in credit card and other financial transaction costs
      related to the collection of ferry fares.  The WSF must notify customers of the surcharge at
      the point of sale and itemize the fee on customer receipts.

      Summary:

      Fuel Taxes.

      Fuel taxes received attributable to the annual inflationary adjustments to the fuel tax rate are
      distributed in the same manner as the non-inflationary 2025 increases in the motor fuel tax
      and special fuel tax rates.

      Retail Sales and Use Taxes on Acquisitions of Luxury Vehicles, and Recreational Vessels.

      The use tax bases for the luxury vehicle tax and recreational vessel tax are modified to align
      with the corresponding sales tax bases, prohibiting the value of any trade-in from being
      deducted from the tax bases.

      Motor homes are exempted from the sales and use taxes on luxury vehicles from July 1,
      2026, through December 31, 2026.

      The following changes are made to the retail sales and use taxes on luxury vehicles:
          •   In the case of a lease requiring periodic payments, the tax may be remitted in equal
              periodic payments over the term of the lease.
          •   The exemptions available under the general retail sales and use tax to enrolled tribal
              members and to nonresidents are extended to the sales and use taxes on luxury
              vehicles.
          •   Motor vehicle dealers are exempt from penalties and interest on any unpaid tax due
              through June 30, 2026.

      Luxury Aircraft Tax.

      An additional 10 percent luxury tax on non-commercial aircraft, which would otherwise
      have taken effect on April 1, 2026, is repealed in its entirety.

      Aircraft Fuel Tax.

      On November 1, 2026, the current aircraft fuel tax is increased from 18 cents per gallon to
      25 cents per gallon.  The 7 cent increase must be deposited into the newly established
      Sustainable Aviation Fuel Airport Infrastructure Account created in the state treasury.

      Aircraft Registration Fee.

      Beginning January 1, 2027, the annual aircraft registration fee is increased from $15 to $30.
      The aircraft registration fee is then increased annually by 2 percent beginning January 1,
      2028.  Increased revenue from the fee increase must be deposited in the Sustainable

      Aviation Fuel Account.

      Aircraft Excise Tax.

      Beginning January 1, 2026, annual excise taxes are raised by a range of $70 to $1,095,
      depending on aircraft type, increasing excise taxes due to a range of $120 to $1,220.

      Beginning January 1, 2028, aircraft excise taxes are then increased by 2 percent each year.

      Commercial unpiloted aircraft systems are made subject to the annual aircraft excise tax.

      The increased amount of annual excise taxes, including the 2 percent annual adjustments, is
      deposited into the Sustainable Aviation Fuel Account.

      Sustainable Aviation Fuel Airport Infrastructure Account.

      The Sustainable Aviation Fuel Airport Infrastructure Account is created in the state
      treasury.  Twenty-eight percent of the revenue collected from aircraft fuel taxes by the
      WSDOT, representing the increases in annual excise tax amounts, must be deposited in this
      account.  These funds are subject to appropriation and may be used only for activities at
      airports supporting sustainable aviation fuel infrastructure improvement and sustainable
      aviation fuel infrastructure preservation projects.

      Tire Fee.

      Clarification is provided that the amount of the tire fee that a retailer is authorized to retain
      is to be 25 cents per tire.


      Registered Tow Truck Operators and the Department of Licensing Reimbursement Program
      for Tows of Indigent Persons' Vehicles.

      The program to reimburse RTTOs for the costs of towing indigent persons' vehicles is
      delayed to July 1, 2027.

      Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle Report.

      The report requirement is discontinued as of November 1, 2025.

      Bus and Bus Facilities Grant Program.

      The bus and bus facilities grant program is expanded to include safety or security
      enhancements for transit rolling stock or transit facilities, except that grant funds may not be
      used for expenses related to armed security.

      Identicards and Older Drivers.

      A program is established requiring the DOL to provide $5 reduced-fee identicards to
      individuals70 years  or older who voluntarily replace their driver's license with an identicard
      at the time the replacement is made.  The DOL must implement this program by January 1,
      2028.

      Toll Collection Communication.

      The WSDOT is authorized to send toll bills and civil penalty notices by email, in addition to
      by paper mail.  The email containing a toll charge bill or a notice of civil penalty must
      prominently indicate that the contents are time sensitive and related to a toll violation.

      Public Transportation Benefit Area Annexations.

      Areas annexed into a PTBA under the annexation process enacted in 2025 are subject to the
      taxes imposed by the PTBA.

      Ferry Debit Card Surcharge.

      The WSF is authorized to recover at least three percent in debit card transaction costs
      related to the collection of ferry fares.

      Account Interest Earnings.

      Interest earnings from the following state accounts must remain with the respective
      accounts:
          •  the Agency Financial Transaction Account:
          •  the Carbon Emissions Reduction Account;
          •  the Climate Commitment Act Transportation Account;
          •  the Complete Streets Grant Program Account;
          •  the Cooper Jones Active Transportation Safety Account;
          •  the Driver Licensing Technology Support Account;
          •  the Ignition Interlock Device Revolving Account;
          •  the Impaired Driving Safety Account;
          •  the Interstate 5 Bridge Replacement Project Toll Facility Bond Retirement Account;
          •  the License Plate Technology Account;
          •  the Marine Fuel Tax Refund Account;
          •  the Recreation Resource Account;
          •  the Sandy Williams Connecting Communities Program Account;
          •  the School Zone Safety Account;
          •  the Sustainable Aviation Fuel Account; and
          •  the Sustainable Aviation Fuel Airport Infrastructure Account.
