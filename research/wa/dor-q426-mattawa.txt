Businesses must collect the appropriate new rate of sales tax for retail sales and services. Persons or
businesses will be subject to the new rate of use tax on items purchased for their personal or business
use if sales tax has not been paid.

Special reporting instructions for sales or leases of motor vehicles
Businesses that report sales or leases of motor vehicles (for up to the ﬁrst 36 months of the lease) will
need to report using the following location codes and tax rates. After the ﬁrst 36 months of motor
vehicle leases, the original city code should be used.
