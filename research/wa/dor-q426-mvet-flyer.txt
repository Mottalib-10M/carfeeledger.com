                    Motor Vehicle
                    Motor vehicle dealers and motor vehicle leasing companies must
                    collect the additional sales tax of five-tenths of one percent (.005)
                    of the selling price. This applies to every retail sale or lease of a
                    motor vehicle in Washington. “Motor vehicle” means every vehicle
                    that is self-propelled as defined in RCW 46.04.320. However, this
                    does not include farm tractors, farm vehicles, offroad vehicles, non-
                    highway vehicles, and snowmobiles.

                    Table abbreviations
                    MVET - Motor Vehicle Excise Tax
                    RTA - Regional Transit Authority
                    HBZ - Hospital Benefit Zone
                    PTBA - Public Transportation Benefit Area

                    sales tax rate changes, visit our website at dor.wa.gov/local-sales-
                    and-use-tax.
