 Calculate vehicle tab fees 
 Calculate your tab renewal fees and learn about what fees we charge.
 Before you get started
 Fees are different for every situation and are calculated in many ways. Everyone starts with the basic fee of $43.25. Things like vehicle weight, location, and taxes determine your final amount. To find out what your specific fees will be, visit a vehicle licensing office location .
