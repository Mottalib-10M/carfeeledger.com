 Motor Vehicle Tax & Fee Schedules

 We are committed to providing efficient, accurate, and courteous service for all your vehicle
 registration, titling, and licensing needs. Our goal is to ensure a smooth and accessible
 experience for every resident.
 For more information on registration fees and requirements, please visit the State of Montana's Motor Vehicle website.
 To get an estimate of the cost to title and register a vehicle, please use the state's online calculator .
 Effective January 1, 2018 : In addition to registration fees,
 an annual luxury tax will be assessed on light vehicles with MSRP over $150,000 [$825.00, MCA 61-3-321(2)(b)] 
 and motorhomes with MSRP over $300,000 [$800.00, MCA 61-3-321(7)(c)], if the vehicle is 10 years old or newer. 
 Tax Schedule for Passenger Vehicles 
 The taxable value for passenger cars, pickup trucks, vans and sport utility vehicles
 previously registered in Montana is based on the manufacturers’ suggested retail price,
 depreciated according to a set schedule as the vehicle ages. The County Option Tax is
 calculated at one half percent of the taxable value of the vehicle (calculators and
 depreciation table below).
 Permanent Registrations 
 Effective January 1, 2006, Motorhomes 11 years old or older may be permanently registered
 at the owner's option.
 Required to register permanently : Trailers (including travel trailers), boats,
 snowmobiles, off highway vehicles, motorcycles, quadricycles.
 Allowed to register permanently if 11 years old or older: Light vehicles, motorhomes, and
 collector vehicles with qualifying collector plate.
 Not allowed to register permanently : Large trucks, light vehicles and motorhomes
 10 years old and newer, vehicles with restricted license plates or exemptions.
 Transfer of ownership : Permanent registrations do not stay with the vehicle.
 Registration ends whenever there is a change in ownership. So, in addition to titling
 fees, new owner(s) must register anew, paying all required taxes and fees. Unless
 required by vehicle type (TL, SN, OHV, BT, MC), a new owner has the option to register
 the vehicle on an annual or permanent basis.
 Late renewals : If an owner wishes to register a qualified vehicle permanently,
 however, has not completed renewal for the current year, and the vehicle was not 11
 years old at the time of scheduled renewal, the owner must complete renewal for that
 registration period before the vehicle can be permanently registered. Since the vehicle
 was not 11 years old in its original renewal period, the vehicle may not be permanently
 registered until vehicle registration is brought current. Then a second registration may
 be processed for permanent registration.
 Permanent registration requests beyond typical renewal period: If a customer has already
 received an annual registration and requests to permanently register before the
 vehicle's next grace period, the customer may register permanently, but no credit is
 given for the fees already paid.
 RPO : Light vehicles will not be allowed permanent registration even if age
 qualified, as those non-resident vehicles that use the RPO process must also verify
 current registration in their home state. An RPO is never issued for a snowmobile, boat,
 or off highway vehicle as other mechanisms provided by FWP exist in statue for
 non-resident use. Motorcycles, quadricycles and trailers (including travel trailers) are
 registered permanently when issued an RPO, as there is no provision in statute to
 register them otherwise.

 Manufactured Homes
 Title fee is $10.30. All property taxes on the manufactured home must be paid in full before a title can be issued or transferred.
 Mobile Homes (Pre-1977) 
 All property taxes must be paid in full. In addition, the owner must complete Montana Department of Revenue 
 Form AB-38 , 
 the Ownership Change Request for a Pre-1977 Mobile Home, and submit it to the Billings field office of DOR via dorprop3A@mt.gov 
 or in person at 175 N 27th Street (Wells Fargo Building), Suite 1102. The Motor Vehicle Department does not work with mobile homes.
 Questions should be directed to DOR via the email above or by calling 406-896-4000, option 2.
