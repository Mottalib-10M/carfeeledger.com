Summary of Common Fees
Summary of Common Fees
Titles
For more information, see Title Requirements in Utah.
 New title – $6.00
 Duplicate title – $6.00
 Title correction, including name change and lien release – $6.00*
* In the event of a title correction, a duplicate registration may also be required.
Registrations
For complete information, see Registration Taxes & Fees, or visit our Fee Estimator- Calculate the Cost of Your Title and Registration tool. If you are looking for your renewal cost, please use our Get Renewal Information tool.
Permits
For more information, see Temporary Permits.
 96-Hour permit – $2.50
 Standard permit – $6.00*
* All applicable title and/or registration fees must be paid at the time of purchase.
Impounds
For more information, see Impound Fees.
Fees shown below are for the impound release only. Towing and storage fees may be assessed by the impound yard where the vehicle is held.
 Abandoned, Accident, Invalid Driver’s License or Theft, LEO/Stop signal Violation – $0.00
 Improper/Expired/No Registration – (Equal to the Registration fee)*
 Driving Under the Influence (DUI) – $455.00
 Boating Under the Influence (BUI) – $455.00
 Ignition Interlock System (IIS) violation – $455.00
 Exhibition Driving – $455.00
 Driver’s license revoked due to drug or alcohol related issues – $455.00
 Revoked No Insurance – $100.00**
 Road rage – $455.00
* Additional title and/or registration fees may apply if registration is not current and correct. Alternative fuel registration fee is applied based on fuel type of vehicle.
**If you provide proof of insurance at time of impound, this fee can be waived.
Disabled Parking Placards
There is no fee associated with new or replacement paper disabled parking placards. There is a $2.50 fee for each durable permanent disabled placard. For complete information, see Disabled Plates and Placards.
