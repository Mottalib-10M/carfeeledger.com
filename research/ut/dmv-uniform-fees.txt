Types of Fees
Utah Code Ann. §59-2-405
One of the following two uniform fees will be due at the time of registration, including the initial purchase, and at each subsequent renewal of registration (once per calendar year). Registration fees also apply.
If you use our Fee Estimator- Calculate the Cost of Your Title and Registration tool, the Uniform Fees will be calculated based off the vehicle information you enter.
Uniform Age-based Fee
The vehicle property assessment fee is a uniform fee based on the age of the vehicle. Vehicles subject to age-based uniform fees include: Passenger vehicles (cars, light trucks, sport utility vehicles, vans), off highway recreational vehicles, street motorcycles, vessels under 31 feet in length, travel trailers, tent trailers, truck campers, non-commercial trailers, small motor vehicles, personal watercraft, and park model recreational vehicles.
Uniform Fee-in-lieu of Property Tax
All other vehicles not listed under the age-based fee above, are subject to the uniform fee in lieu of property tax, which is 1.5 or 1.0 percent of the fair market value of vehicles as established by the Tax Commission. Subject to the 1.5 percent fee-in-lieu are medium and heavy duty trucks, commercial trailers, and vessels 31 feet and longer. Subject to the 1.0 percent fee-in-lieu are motor homes.
