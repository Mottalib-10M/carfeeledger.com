The Utah Road Usage Charge Program offers two mileage reporting options: telematics and odometer photo. With telematics, the vehicle automatically records and reports mileage data. With odometer photo, customers take a picture of their odometer every 3 months using a mobile device. Neither option involves sharing location data.

Drive as usual! Nothing else is required. For more information about road usage charging, please visit our Road Usage Charge Program page.
EV drivers can choose to continue to pay the flat fee for alternative fuel vehicles or enroll in Utah’s Road Usage Charge program to pay for road usage based on the number of miles they drive, up to the amount of the set flat fee. The flat fee applicable to each 12-month vehicle registration period beginning in 2026 is $180. For example, someone with a vehicle registration period beginning on July 1, 2026 and who is enrolled in the Road Usage Charge program would pay mileage fees at a rate of 1.25 cents per mile up to a $180 cap from July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. To enroll, you will create an account for which you will need your vehicle information and a valid credit or debit card to set up a prepaid wallet. You will sign a user agreement and select a mileage reporting option. Depending on your vehicle, you may choose between telematics and odometer photo reporting options. Once your account is created and vehicle enrolled, 1.25 cents per mile (the 2026 calendar year mileage rate) will be deducted from your prepaid wallet, up to the amount of the flat fee.
