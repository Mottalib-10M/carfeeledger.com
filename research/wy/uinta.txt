Wyoming Statutes dictate the calculation of all registration fees. Therefore, the fees are the same no matter what county a vehicle is registered in. However, the county that collects the fees keeps and distributes the money to the taxing entities in their county. Therefore, residents are encourages to register vehicles in the county where they reside. Registration fees consist of two parts: a county fee and a state fee. County Fees The county fee is a tax based on the value of the vehicle and is calculated using the following formula: Factory Cost x Year of Service Rate x 0.03 County Fee A new vehicle having a factory cost of $20,000 would have a county fee of: $20,000 X 60% X .03 = $360.00 Calculator Year of Vehicle: 2023 or Newer 2022 2021 2020 2019 2018 or older MSRP: Type of Vehicle: Car, Van, SUV Truck House Trailer Other Trailer Motorcycle Weight (if truck or trailer): 1,000 Pounds or Less 1,001 - 3,500 3,501 - 4,500 4,501 - 5,500 5,501 - 6,000 6,001 or more This calculator provides an estimate of costs based on the information provided by the user, and should be used for informational purposes only. Factory Cost or MSRP The factory cost is the original cost placed on vehicle by the automobile factory. The year of service rate is determined by how many years the vehicle has been registered for use. Service rates are as follows: 1st Year - 60% 2nd Year - 50% 3rd Year - 40% 4th Year - 30% 5th Year - 20% 6th Year and After - 15% ​ State Fees The state fee is a fee levied by the State of Wyoming and is remitted to the Department of Transportation each month.
