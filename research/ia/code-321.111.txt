1 MOTOR VEHICLES AND L A W OF THE ROAD, §321.111 321.111 Conversion of car — effect. Any motor vehicle originally registered as a passenger car and thereafter converted into a truck with a loading capacity of less than one thousand pounds, shall be registered as a passenger car .
