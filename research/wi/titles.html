Wisconsin DMV accepts the following forms of payment:

  • Credit cards accepted: MasterCard, Visa, American Express, Discover
  • Debit card (processed as credit)
  • PayPal and Apple Pay is available with some online services
    • NOTE: Your credit or debit card will be charged a convenience fee for this service
  • ACH (Automated Clearing House) from your checking or savings account
    • Checks made payable to "Registration Fee Trust"
  • Cash option available ONLY at DMV Customer Service Centers. Do NOT mail cash
  • Fees collected support the Department of Transportation budget
