DMV customer service centers Forms Drivers Content_Area2 Act 181: $6 Per Plate Issuance Fee On April 4, 2026 WI passed Act 181 (formerly Assembly Bill 387) giving DMV the authority to collect the "fee of $6 per plate for the issuance of new registration plates that are not replacement plates" originally described in Section 329 of Act 15. The WI DMV began collecting the new issuance on Monday, April 13, 2026. Act 53: Heavy Fleet License Plate On December 9, 2025 WI passed Act 53 allowing fleet registration for heavy trucks of up to 54,000 pounds and heavy trailers of up to 80,000 pounds. Implementation details are being determined. Act 50: Personalized Gold Star License Plate Fee On December 9, 2025 WI passed Act 50 exempting a Gold Star family member from the personalized license plate fee for a Gold Star license plate. The personalized plate fee waiver will begin on July 1, 2026. Act 15: 2025-2027 Biennial Budget On July 3, 2025 WI passed Act 15 adopting its biennial budget for 2025-2027. There are several items in Act 15 which affect motor vehicles. The new fees will be effective October 1, 2025. Driver Fee Increases SECTION 344. 343.21 (1) (a) of Act 15 increases the original issuance and renewal of the Class D driver license from $24 to $32.50. This does not include the card issuance fee of $10. Vehicle Fee Increases Section 329 of Act 15 establishes "a fee of $6 per plate for the issuance of new registration plates that are not replacement plates." Sections 338 and 339 of Act 15 increases a replacement plate fee from $4 to $6. Section 341 of Act 15 increases the annual fees in 341.25 (c) to (q) by 10%. The annual fees in 341.25 (c) to (q) are used to calculate most registration fees from 8,000 lbs to 80,000 lbs. Sections 342 and 343 of Act 15 increases the title fee for a vehicle from $157 to $207.
