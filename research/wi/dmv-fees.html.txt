<h3>Wisconsin DMV accepts the following forms of payment&#58;</h3><ul><li>Credit/Debit cards*&#58; MasterCard, Visa, Discover (and American Express in person at DMV Service Centers)</li><li>PayPal* is available with some online services</li><li>Apple Pay* is available with some online services</li><li>ACH (Automated Clearing House) deducts fees directly from your checking or savings account with no added convenience/service fee</li><li>Checks made payable to &quot;Registration Fee Trust&quot;</li><li>Cash option is available ONLY at DMV Customer Service Centers.
