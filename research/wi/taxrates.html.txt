   <strong>What is the sales tax rate?</strong></p><p>The Wisconsin sales tax is a 5% tax imposed on the sales price of retailers who sell, license, lease, or rent tangible personal property, certain coins and stamps, certain leased property affixed to realty, or certain digital goods, or sell, license, perform, or furnish taxable services in Wisconsin. See 
   <a href="/Pages/FAQS/ise-taxable.aspx">What is Taxable</a> for more information.</p><p id="txrate3">3. 
   <strong>What is the use tax rate?</strong></p><p>The Wisconsin use tax is a 5% tax imposed on the purchase price of tangible personal property, certain coins and stamps, certain leased property affixed to realty, certain digital goods, or taxable services that are used, stored, or consumed within Wisconsin, but upon which a Wisconsin sales or use tax has not previously been paid. See 
   <a href="/Pages/FAQS/ise-usetax.aspx#use1">Use Tax</a> for more information.</p><p id="txrate4">4. 
   <strong>What is the county tax rate?</strong></p><p>Seventy Wisconsin counties have adopted a 0.5% county tax. Milwaukee County sales and use tax increases from 0.5% to 0.9%, effective January 1, 2024. </p><p>County tax is imposed on the sales price from retail sales, licenses, leases, or rentals of tangible personal property, certain coins and stamps, certain leased property affixed to realty, certain digital goods, or the selling, licensing, performing, or furnishing of taxable services in a county with a county tax. Exceptions to the county tax treatment and special situations are explained in 
   <a href="/DOR%20Publications/pb201.pdf">Publication 201</a>, 
   <em>Wisconsin Sales and Use Tax Information</em>, Part 18.D.</p><p id="txrate5">5. 
   <strong>What is the city tax rate?</strong></p><p>The city of Milwaukee is the only municipality that imposes a city sales and use tax. The 2% city of Milwaukee sales and use tax is effective January 1, 2024. </p><p>City tax is imposed on the sales price from retail sales, licenses, leases, or rentals of tangible personal property, certain coins and stamps, certain leased property affixed to realty, certain digital goods, or the selling, licensing, performing, or furnishing of taxable services to a location in the city of Milwaukee.</p><p id="txrate6">6. 
   <strong>Which sales and use tax rates apply?</strong></p><p>Use our 
   <a href="/Pages/Apps/strb.aspx">Wisconsin State and Local Tax Rate Look-up</a> to find rates that may apply. The rate chart below provides Wisconsin's state, county, and city sales and use tax rates. See Publication 201, 
   <em>Wisconsin Sales and Use Tax Information</em>, Part 7.C for more information on where a sale takes place. </p><p>Regardless of where the retailer is located, a retailer registered to collect Wisconsin sales and use tax must also collect county tax and city tax if the retailer&#58;</p><ul class="listLinks"><li>Makes taxable sales that are sourced to (i.e., take place in) a county that adopted the county tax,</li><li>Makes taxable sales that are sourced to a city that adopted a city tax, or</li><li>Sells a motor vehicle, boat, recreational vehicle, or aircraft that will be customarily kept in a county that adopted the county tax or a city that adopted a city tax.</li></ul><p> 
   <strong>Note&#58;</strong> Information on which county tax is imposed on sales and leases of motor vehicles, boats, recreational vehicles, aircraft, snowmobiles, trailers, semitrailers, all-terrain vehicles, utility vehicles, and off-highway motorcycles is provided in Publication 201,
