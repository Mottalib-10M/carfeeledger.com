   <a href="https&#58;//docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/related/acts/181">Act 181</a> (formerly Assembly Bill 387) giving DMV the authority to collect the &quot;fee of $6 per plate for the issuance of new registration plates that are not replacement plates&quot; originally described in Section 329 of Act 15. The WI DMV began collecting the new issuance on Monday, April 13, 2026.<br></p><h2>Act 53&#58; Heavy Fleet License Plate</h2><p>On December 9, 2025 WI passed 
   <a href="https&#58;//docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/related/acts/53">Act 53</a> allowing fleet registration for heavy trucks of up to 54,000 pounds and heavy trailers of up to 80,000 pounds. Implementation details are being determined.</p><h2>Act 50&#58; Personalized Gold Star License Plate Fee</h2><p>On December 9, 2025 WI passed 	
   <a href="https&#58;//docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/related/acts/50">Act 50</a> exempting a Gold Star family member from the personalized license plate fee for a Gold Star license plate. The personalized plate fee waiver will begin on July 1, 2026.</p><h2>Act 15&#58; 2025-2027 Biennial Budget</h2><p>On July 3, 2025 WI passed 
   <a href="https&#58;//docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/related/acts/15">Act 15</a> adopting its biennial budget for 2025-2027.  There are several items in 
   <a href="https&#58;//docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/2025/related/acts/15">Act 15</a> which affect motor vehicles. The new fees will be effective October 1, 2025.</p><div class="accordion" id="accordionOne"><div class="accordion-item"><h2 class="accordion-header">
