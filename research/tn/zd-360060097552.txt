VR-6 - New Residents Should Register Within 30 Days
https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360060097552-VR-6-New-Residents-Should-Register-Within-30-Days
updated 2021-04-28T20:52:48Z
A person who moves to Tennessee from another state should register their vehicle within 30 days of the move. There are limited exceptions to this provision, including when students from other states attend schools in Tennessee.
Reference: Tenn. Code Ann. § 55-4-101(b)(2).
