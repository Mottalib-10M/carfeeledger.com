 Local Sales Tax Rates
 The map below shows sales tax rates for each county. Once you hover over a county, local sales tax rates for localities within that county also appear, along with additional information, including effective dates, FIPS codes, situs codes, and food tax rates.
 * Denotes a city with boundaries in more than one county.
 **There is an 0.5% local option transit surcharge in Davidson County beginning February 1, 2025. This effectively increases the local sales and food tax rates to 2.75%.
