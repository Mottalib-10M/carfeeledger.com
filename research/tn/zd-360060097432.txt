The standard plate fee for a passenger vehicle is $26.50. The standard plate fee for a Motorcycle is $19.50. For information about additional fees for specific Specialty/Cultural plates click here. For information about additional fees for Personalized plates click here. Check with your local county clerk’s office for additional fees for vehicle registration.
