LIEN-10 - Fee to Note a Lien
https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360060050212-LIEN-10-Fee-to-Note-a-Lien
updated 2021-04-28T20:25:11Z
The fees for noting a lien at the county clerks office are:
$11 for noting of lien;
$11 title fee; and
Any applicable county processing fees, that vary.
