VTR-34 - Sales Tax on a Vehicle Purchase
https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360060395571-VTR-34-Sales-Tax-on-a-Vehicle-Purchase
updated 2026-09-30T13:03:10Z
Purchasers of new and used vehicles must pay state sales tax at the rate of 7 percent and the state single-article tax at the rate of 2.75 percent. Please keep in mind that each county may also have a wheel tax and local tax, which vary depending on the county.
