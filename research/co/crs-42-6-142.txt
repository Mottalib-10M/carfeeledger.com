Section 42-6-142
Penalties
 (1)
A person shall not sell, transfer, or in any manner dispose of a motor or off-highway vehicle in this state without complying with this part 1. 
 (2)
A person who violates subsection (1) of this section for which no other penalty is expressly provided commits a petty offense.
