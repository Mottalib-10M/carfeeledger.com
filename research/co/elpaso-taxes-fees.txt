 Late Fees
 | 
 Late fees may apply when a registration renewal is not done within the proper time. Visit the Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles website for more information.
 Motor Vehicles 
Registration of a newly purchased vehicle is due within 60 days of purchase. Late fees will apply if the vehicle is not registered 61 days after purchase. Late fees are $25 per month, with the total amount not exceeding $100 (four months). Renewal of your registration is due in the month of expiration. A grace period of one month after the expiration is allowed. This grace period does not apply to temporary permits. A late fee will apply registration renewals after the one-month grace period. The late fee is $25.00 per month, with the total amount not exceeding $100 (four months).
 Non-Motive Power Vehicles 
This includes trailers, camper trailers, multipurpose trailers, trailer coaches and special mobile machinery, that weigh up to 16,000 pounds and are taxable. For any type of trailer, this is the empty weight.
 The late fee on non-motive power vehicles is $10 total, effective July 1, 2010. When there are late fees accrued prior to that date, those fees are added to the late fees incurred after July 1, 2010. These late fees may not exceed the limit of $100 total. Late fees on commercial and farm vehicle trailers could be reduced or waived if these it’s been idle for a full registration period. |
