E-Check: Vehicle emissions testing for Cuyahoga, Geauga, Lake, Lorain, Medina, Portage, and Summit counties.
New Ohio Legislation - HB54 (Effective June 30, 2025) Expanded New Vehicle Exemptions. Exemption period extended: Gas and diesel vehicles: 6 years; Non-plug-in hybrids: 7 years. Emissions testing for 2026 begins for: Model year 2020 gas and diesel vehicles; Model year 2019 non-plug-in hybrids. Test certificates are valid for 365 days.
Gasoline and diesel vehicles manufactured in even years from 2002-2020, non-plug-in Hybrids from 2002-2019, and less than or equal to 10,000 lbs (gross vehicle weight rating), will need an E-Check for registration renewal during 2026.
Ohio's vehicle test program requires emissions testing for vehicles from six, and hybrids from seven, through 26 years old. In 2026, all the vehicles from even model years and any odd model transfers (purchases) without a current passing test will be tested using the Onboard Diagnostics Second Generation (OBDII) test.
