 DSMV FAQs
 How long do I have to register a motor vehicle? 
 You have 60 days from the date of purchase to register your vehicle.
 What documents do I need to register my vehicle? 
 Title or Manufacturers Statement of Origin
 Bill of Sale
 Lien Contract (retail installment contract)
 Odometer Statement
 Proof of Assessment
 Proof of Property Taxes Paid Proof of Insurance
 What documents do I need to register my vehicle if I just moved to Arkansas? 
 Title
 Odometer Statement
 OR (if you have a lien on the vehicle, and the lien holder has the title) Out of State Registration
 What is the sales tax rate for a motor vehicle? 
 New Vehicle – 6.5%
 Used Vehicle – $4,000 through $9,999 will be 3.5% Used Vehicle – $3,999 and below no sales tax due
 Do I need a title to register a boat? 
 If the year of the boat is 2020 or newer, Arkansas will issue a title. Before 2020, there will only be a registration.
 If I purchased a vehicle from a dealer that does not issue temporary tags, how do I get a temporary tag? 
 You can visit any State Revenue Office in Arkansas with your bill of sale and driver’s license to be issued a temporary tag. A temporary tag must be issued within the first 10 days after purchase of the motor vehicle.
 What is the penalty if I do not get my motor vehicle registered before the 60 days is up? 
 There will be a 10% sales tax penalty of the total sales tax due. Penalty for late registration is $3.00 each 10 days the motor vehicle is not registered.
 What do I need to do if I lost my title? 
 You can visit a State Revenue Office and apply for a duplicate title for $10.00.
 Do I need to retest for my driver’s license if I let it expire? 
 You will not need to retest for an expired Class D driver’s license. If it is a CDL, you can renew it without testing as long as it has not been expired for 1 year or longer.
 What do I need to get a Real ID? 
 You will need:
 Proof of Legal Presence
 Social Security Card or DD214
 Proof of Identity (2) Proof of Residency
 Is there a way to get the title for my car fast? 
 Yes, you can apply in person at the Central Revenue Office in Little Rock. There will be an additional $10 fee for an expedited title. The title will be available for pick up in 3 business days.
 What do I need to get an Arkansas Identification/Driver’s License? 
 You will need your Proof of Legal Presence, Social Security Card, and out of state license or ID (if applicable)
 Can I get a Real ID at any State Revenue Office? 
 Yes, Real ID is available at any State Revenue Office in Arkansas.
 What do I need to renew my Real ID? 
 You will not need to bring in any documentation as long as you are not changing name or address.
