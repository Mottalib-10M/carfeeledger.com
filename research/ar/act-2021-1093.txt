29                           (A)(i)  Is propelled by an electric motor powered by a

31                           (B)(ii)  Is not propelled by an internal combustion
32    engine;.
33                           (B)  "Electric vehicle" does not include:
34                                   (i)  A golf cart;
35                                   (ii)  A low-speed vehicle;
36                                   (iii)  An electric motorcycle; or
