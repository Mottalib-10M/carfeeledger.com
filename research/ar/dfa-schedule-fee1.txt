   When transferring a current license plate to a higher weight class, the difference between the two registration fees
   will be charged. For example, the tag is registered to a vehicle that weighs 2,800 lbs. with a $17.00 registration fee
   and is being transferred to a higher weight class vehicle that weighs 3,400 lbs. with a $25.00 registration fee. In
   addition to the $1.00 transfer fee, $8.00 will be charged for the difference in the weight classes. No refunds are
   given when going down in weight class.
