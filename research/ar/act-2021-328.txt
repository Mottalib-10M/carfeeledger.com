27            SECTION 1.  Arkansas Code § 27-14-614 is amended to read as follows:
28            27-14-614.  Additional fee for electric vehicles and hybrid vehicles —
29    Definitions — Exception.
30            (a)  As used in this section:
31                    (1)  “Electric vehicle” means a vehicle that:
32                           (A)  Is propelled by an electric motor powered by a battery
33    or other electrical device incorporated into the vehicle; and
34                           (B)  Is not propelled by an internal combustion engine; and
35                    (2)  “Hybrid vehicle” means a vehicle that draws propulsion
36    energy from both an internal combustion engine and an energy storage device.
