Documentation Preparation Fee Adjustment in 2026 
 
After consultation and advice from industry leaders, and upon review of IC 9-32-13-7(b), the 
Division advises that it will not take enforcement action related to documentation preparation 
fees at or below $261.72 as of July 1, 2026. As indicated in its September 15, 2022 memo, the 
Division bases this figure on the Consumer Price Index calculator provided by the federal Bureau 
of Labor Statistics1, adjusted for inflation as of May 2026.  
 
As a reminder, documentation preparation fees are to be explicitly included in the advertised sale 
price of the vehicle and must be affirmatively disclosed in writing during the sale transaction and 
as a standalone line item on a bill of sale.

1 The calculator is available online here: https://www.bls.gov/data/inflation_calculator.htm
