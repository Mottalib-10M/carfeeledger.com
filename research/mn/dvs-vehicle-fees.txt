Vehicle fees

There are fees associated with different motor vehicle transactions. These include transfer transactions, title transactions, plate fees and fees associated with registration.

To verify fees, visit a motor vehicle office or contact Motor Vehicle Services at[pic.dvs@cx.mn.gov](mailto:pic.dvs@cx.mn.gov).

Learn more about taxes:




Listed below are different fees that may apply to specific title or license plate transactions.

## A note about fees

Fees are set and distributed by the Minnesota Legislature.
