DMV reservations scheduled for the week of February 23 - February 27 will be honored through the month of March.
Expiration Date Extensions Due to February Blizzard
Due to the State of Emergency declared by Governor Daniel J. McKee following the February 22–23, 2026 blizzard, the Rhode Island Division of Motor Vehicles is extending certain expiration dates.
New Expiration Date: March 31, 2026
The following are extended:
Driver’s licenses
, permits, and identification cards that were set to expire between
February 1, 2026 and February 28, 2026. This extension does not include non-domiciled commercial driver’s licenses or commercial learner’s permits.
Vehicle registrations
that were set to expire on
February 28, 2026.
Inspection stickers
that were set to expire on
February 28, 2026.
Disability parking placards
that were set to expire between
February 1, 2026 and February 28, 2026.
If your expiration date falls within the ranges listed above, it has been extended to March 31, 2026.

Plate Fees
Acceptable forms of payment are: cash, money orders, personal checks, bank checks, and credit card (MasterCard, Discover, Visa and American Express). Checks should be payable to Division of Motor Vehicles.
Third-party and temporary/starter checks are not accepted.
All fees listed below include $3.50 technology surcharge. If you are paying by credit card, there is a minimum service fee of $1.55 per transaction, or 2.40% for transactions of $65.00 and over. This fee goes to the third party provider, Federal Payments, and is in addition to any fees listed below.
Effective July 1, 2013, Rhode Island licenses and motor vehicle registrations are subject to surcharges as part of the
Transportation Investment and Debt Reduction Act of 2011
.
