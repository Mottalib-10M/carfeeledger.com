Fraudulent Text Alert
Updated 4/11/2026. The Rhode Island Division of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is alerting the public following a recent surge in fraudulent text messages falsely claiming to be from the DMV. These scams are designed to deceive recipients into clicking malicious links and submitting personal and/or financial information under false threats of license suspension, fines, and credit score or legal penalties. These messages are NOT from the RI DMV.

SALES TAX INFORMATION FOR GIFTED VEHICLES
Immediate Family:
No sales tax on the vehicle unless outstanding taxes are due. A signed letter of gift is required to exempt you from the sales tax.
Immediate family members for purposes of taxation are:  parent, step-parent, sibling, step-sibling, spouse, child or step-child.
Non-Immediate Family:
No sales tax on the vehicle unless outstanding taxes are due. A signed and notarized letter of gift and gift affidavit is required to exempt you from the sales tax
