This bulletin supersedes Bulletin 2023-01-CF, issued January 13, 2023. 

Section 13(2)(a) of the Motor Vehicle Sales Finance Act, MCL 492.113(2)(a), states in relevant part: 

The documentary preparation fee shall not exceed 5% of the cash price of the motor vehicle or $160.00, whichever is less. Beginning on January 1, 2005, the [Director] shall adjust the $160.00 maximum of the documentary preparation fee described in the subsection every 2 years to reflect the cumulative percentage change in the consumer price index of the 2 immediately preceding calendar years, as determined by the [Director]. The [Director] shall round the adjustment to the nearest $10.00 increment to set the fee every 2 years … 

The Director has reviewed the documentary preparation fee adjustment and has determined the maximum shall be adjusted to $280.00, which reflects the cumulative percentage change in the consumer price index for the calendar years 2023 and 2024. Accordingly, the documentary preparation fee shall not exceed 5% of the cash price of the motor vehicle or $280.00, whichever is less.
