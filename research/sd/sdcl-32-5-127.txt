32-5-127. Decals and plates mailed to owner--Fee.
In addition to the registration fees required by this chapter, the department or county treasurer shall charge the owner, when sent to the owner through the mail:
(1) Two dollars and fifty cents per decal or set of decals; and
(2) Twelve dollars per license plate or set of plates.
 
Source: SL 1994, ch 240, § 5; SL 2009, ch 150, § 1; SL 2023, ch 98, § 2, eff. Mar. 14, 2023; SL 2026, ch 129, § 2.
