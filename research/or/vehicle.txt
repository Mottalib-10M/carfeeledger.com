Oregon does not have a separate charge for duplicate titles. The title fee chart below applies to duplicate and title transfers. Please make checks and money orders payable to Oregon DMV. 
   Titling Vehicle
      Calculate fees and prepare your title application online if you have a passenger vehicle.
How To Apply
Make sure you have 
               all required title documents.
Apply by mail to DMV Services, 1905 Lana Ave NE, Salem, OR 97314 or 
               make an appointment at a DMV office to complete your transaction.
                  Late title transfers (occurring between 31 – 60 days) incur a fee of $25.*
                  Late title transfers (occurring after 60 days) incur a fee of $50.*
*A late title transfer fee is due if you apply for a title more than 30 days after the sale of an Oregon-titled vehicle.
NOT Titling Vehicle
      You have an Oregon title in your name, and need to renew or replace plates:
Apply to renew and/or replace your plates 
               online for most plates. There's no need to calculate your fees. See “Miscellaneous Fees” on this page.
         You have an Oregon title in your name, but no plates on vehicle or cannot renew and/or replace online:
 Visit our 
               License Plates page to calculate your fees.
Send a completed 
               application to DMV Services, 1905 Lana Ave NE, Salem OR 97314.
								Title Fees
							​You can calculate the fees for passenger vehicles online on DMV2U. ​​​
Visit our 
      Titling and Registering Your Vehicle page to find out what documents you need to apply for title.
DMV uses the MPG rating assigned to the VIN, as provided by the vehicle manufacturer. You can find your vehicle’s combined MPG rating at 
      fueleconomy.gov. 
      ​Title fees apply to original, duplicate, and replacement transactions.
​Title fees for passenger vehicles and trucks (26,000 pounds GVWR or less):
If the vehicle has a combined rating of 0 – 19 MPG, the fee is $101.​
If the vehicle has a combined rating of 20 –​ 39 MPG, the fee is $106.
If the vehicle has a combined rating of 40 MPG or higher, the fee is $116.
If the vehic​le is all electric, the fee is $192.
      ​​O​​ther title fees include:
      If the vehicle is a Light Trailer, Travel Trailer, Motorcycle, Moped, Motor Home, Bus, Camper, Park Model RV or ATV, the fee is $101. All-electric vehicles are subject to the electric vehicle title fee above, regardless of vehicle type.​
The heavy vehicle title fee is $90. 
                  ​Heavy vehicles are motor vehicles with a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) over 26,000 pounds and trailers with a loaded weight over 8,000 pounds. Excludes motor homes, special use trailers, and travel trailers.
The salvage title fee is $27.
Late title transfers (occurring between 31 – 60 days) incur a fee of $25.*
Late title transfers (occurring after 60 days) incur a fee of $50.*
      *A late title transfer fee is due if you apply for a title more than 30 days after the sale of an Oregon-titled vehicle.​​
								Registration and Renewal Fees
							​Passenger Vehicles​​
DMV uses the MPG rating assigned to the VIN, as provided by the vehicle manufacturer. You can find your vehicle’s combined MPG rating at 
      fueleconomy.gov.  
      REGULAR PASSENGER REGISTRATION is valid for two years.
      NEW PASSENGER VEHICLE with a Manufacturer’s Certificate of Origin: Receives four-year registration. Double the Registration/Renewal AND any county fees.
      Add together each fee type that applies to your situation to get your total fee.
      Registration and renewal fees are due when the vehicle has no Oregon plates or the Oregon plates are expired.
         For two-year registration: ​
If the passenger vehicle has a combined rating of 0 – 19 MPG, the fee is $126.
If the passenger vehicle has a combined rating of 20 - 39 MPG, the fee is $136.
If the passenger vehicle has a combined rating of 40 MPG or higher and is not enrolled in OReGO, the fee is $216*.
If the passenger vehicle is all electric and not enrolled in OReGO, the fee is $376*.
If the vehicle is a motorcycle or moped, the fee is $88.
*OReGO​ is a road usage charge program where drivers pay for the miles they drive, instead of the per-gallon gas tax. If you enroll in OReGO the two-year registration fee is $86.​
Starting July 1, 2027, when it is time to renew, drivers of used electric vehicles must pay a $86 two-year registration fee, and decide to EITHER pay a flat fee or ​pay by the mile. This will go into effect Jan. 1, 2028, for new electric vehicles and July 1, 2028, for hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles.​
      ​Other registration and renewal fees may be due if applicable to your situation:
If you need new license plates for your passenger vehicle at initial registration, the fee is $26.
If you are moving Oregon plates onto another vehicle, the fee is $30.
               You will also owe any applicable registration and county fees if you do not own the vehicle the plates were removed from.
If you live in, and/or the vehicle stays in, Multnomah County, the county fee is $112. 
​​If you live in, and/or the vehicle stays in, Washington or Clackamas County, the county fee is $60.
      Fees for special background plates (you and/or the vehicle must qualify for the plate): Visit our 
      License Plates page​.
   Other Vehicle Types
For non-passenger vehicles, check the 
      Vehicle Types page for title and registration guidelines specific to your vehicle. Visit our 
      License Plates page to calculate special plate fees.
								Miscellaneous Fees
							Permit Fees
The fee for a light vehicle trip permit (valid for 21 days) is $35.
The fee for a heavy motor vehicle trip permit (valid for 10 days) is $43.
The fee for a heavy trailer trip permit (valid for 10 days) is $10.
The fee for a recreational vehicle trip permit (valid for 1- 10 days) is $35.
The fee for a registered vehicle trip permit (valid for 10 days) is $7.50.
The fee for a registration weight trip permit (valid for 10 days) is $5.
There is no fee for a disabled person parking permit.
The fee for a snowmobile trip permit (valid for 60 days) is $7.
​The snowmobile trip permit is only available to non-Oregon residents.
Sno-Park Fees:​
A daily permit is $4.
A 3-day permit is $9.
An annual permit is $25.
Replacement/Plate/VIN Fees
For information on special background plates, visit the License Plates page​ and review the PDF under “Fees and How to Apply.”
​A vehicle identification number (VIN) inspection done in Oregon is $9.
For a two-plated vehicle, the plate fee is $26.
For a one-plated vehicle, the plate fee is $13.
The fee to replace a plate or plates, with or without stickers, is $12.
This fee is in addition to plate fees.
The fee to replace stickers only is $10.​
