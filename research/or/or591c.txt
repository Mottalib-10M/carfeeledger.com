General instructions
The vehicle use tax is 0.5 percent of the retail sales price 
of taxable vehicles sold by a dealer outside of Oregon. A 
taxable vehicle is one with up to 7 ,500 miles and a gross 
vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of 26,000 lbs. or less. If 
the vehicle doesn’t have an odometer, it’s taxable if it’s 
sold with a manufacturer’s certificate of origin (MCO) 
or manufacturer’s statement of origin (MSO). For more 
information about which vehicle sales qualify, visit 
  www.oregon.gov /dor.
Once we have received and processed your return and 
payment, we will send you a certificate of vehicle use 
tax paid that you need to finish registering the vehicle 
with the Department of Transportation, Driver and 
Motor Vehicle Services (DMV). You can choose to have 
the certificate sent by email or by standard mail.
What is the applicable law? Oregon Revised Statutes 
(ORS) 320.400–320.485.
Form instructions
Filer type.  Check if you are filing as a business with 
and FEIN or and individual or sole proprietor with an 
SSN.
Sole Proprietor
Individuals. (If sole proprietor using SSN).  Enter your 
legal name.
Social Security number (SSN). If you don’t have a FEIN, 
enter your SSN.
Business
Businesses. Enter your business name as you filed it 
with the Oregon Secretary of State.  
Federal employer identification number (FEIN).   This 
is a nine-digit number assigned by the IRS. Enter your 
FEIN in this box.
Doing business as (DBA). If you’re doing business under 
a different name than the name above, enter that name 
in this box.
Date vehicle purchased. Enter the date the vehicle was 
purchased.
Mailing address. Enter your mailing address, city, state, 
and ZIP code.
Email address. If you want to receive an email with the 
certificate showing the use tax has been paid, check the 
box next to “Email my proof of tax paid certificate” and 
provide your email address in the “Contact email” space. 
Once the return and payment have been processed, the 
certificate will be sent to the email address provided.
If you don’t check the box, your certificate will be printed 
and mailed.
Due date. The return and payment are due 30 calendar 
days following the date the vehicle was purchased.
Penalty. If you don’t pay the tax by the due date, you’ll 
be charged 5 percent of the unpaid tax. If you fail to file 
within 30 days of the due date, an additional penalty of 
20 percent of the unpaid tax will be due.
Interest. Interest is charged on any unpaid tax from 
the due date until the date payment in full is received. 
The current interest rate can be found on our website at 
 www.oregon.gov /dor.
Vehicle information instructions
Enter the make, year, vehicle identification number 
(VIN), state and country where the vehicle was pur -
chased. Please make sure the VIN is legible, as that will 
be the number listed on your certificate.
If you’re filing a return for a vehicle that doesn’t have a 
VIN, enter the serial number. 
Line instructions
Line 1. Enter the cash sales price. The cash sales price is 
not reduced by trade-in allowance or down payments, 
and does not include charges for document fees, regis -
tration fees, optional service contracts or extended war-
ranties, gap insurance, etc. It does include any charges 
for transportation of the vehicle before its sale. 
Line 2. Enter the total of any dealer rebates or discounts.
Line 3. Enter the total of any add-ons. Add-ons include 
accessories, parts, or other products that are sold with a 
vehicle, as well as any charges included for installation 
or application labor, that are not included in the cash 
sales price.
Line 4.  Subtract line 2 from line 1, then add line 3 to 
calculate the taxable sale amount.
Line 5. Current tax rate.
Line 6.  Multiply the taxable sale amount on line 4 by 
0.005.

Line 7. Enter the total of any privilege, excise, sales, or 
use taxes paid to another jurisdiction that are separately 
stated on the sales documents. Only include taxes cal -

Line 8. Subtract line 7 from line 6. This is your vehicle

your check or money order to this return.
