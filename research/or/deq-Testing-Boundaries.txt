Is your vehicle required to pass the emissions test?
Oregon's federally enforced 
 State Implementation Plan requires that non-exempt passenger vehicles are tested, every two years, in the Portland and Medford metropolitan areas. This testing is part of the state's compliance with current standards for 
 Criteria Air Pollutants. 
Your renewal registration form mailed to you from the DMV may state if your vehicles needs to test.
To determine if your vehicle is registered within a DEQ emissions testing boundary visit our VIP Boundary Lookup tool below:
