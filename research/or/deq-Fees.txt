Portland area Clean Air Station fees
Clackamas, Gresham, Northeast, Sherwood and Sunset Stations 
Vehicle Testing Fee: 
In the Portland Area, the vehicle testing fee is 
 $25. This fee is due only after your vehicle receives a passing test result, and is issued a Certificate of Compliance.
There is no charge if your vehicle fails.
Payment options include Cash, PIN-less Debit/Credit card (VISA, MasterCard or Discover) or money order accepted.
Vehicle Registration Fees: 
Although the Vehicle Inspection Program is authorized to collect vehicle registration fees as part of registration renewals, these fees are separate from vehicle testing fees. These State (DMV) and County fees, which are subject to change, are linked below.

Medford Area Clean Air Station Fees
Vehicle Testing Fee: 
The Medford Area vehicle testing fee is 
 $20. This fee is due only after your vehicle receives a passing test result, and is issued a Certificate of Compliance.
There is no charge if your vehicle fails.
Payment options include Cash, PIN-less Debit/Credit card (VISA, MasterCard or Discover) or money order accepted.
Vehicle Registration Fees: 
Although the Vehicle Inspection Program is authorized to collect vehicle registration fees as part of registration renewals, these fees are separate from vehicle testing fees. The State (DMV) fees, which are subject to change, are linked below. 
 Department of Motor Vehicle's Registration Fees 
DEQ Too Fees:
Vehicle Testing Fee: 
The DEQ testing fees are the same as those charged by Clean Air Stations, 
 $25 in the Portland Area, and 
 $20 in the Medford area. This fee is due only after your vehicle receives a passing test result, and is issued a Certificate of Compliance.
There is no charge if your vehicle fails.
Payment options vary by location and include Cash and Debit/Credit card (VISA, MasterCard or Discover).
Some DEQ Too locations may or may not charge an additional Device-Usage or service fee.
