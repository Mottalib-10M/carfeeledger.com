Roads Travel Map Breadcrumb Home Motor Vehicle What to Bring to Your Motor Vehicle Appointment What to Bring to Your Motor Vehicle Appointment Having the right documents with you helps your motor vehicle appointment go quickly and smoothly. Below you'll find what to bring for the most common transactions, including buying a vehicle, updating your vehicle information, transferring a vehicle to North Dakota, and renewing or registering a vehicle. Requirements vary by transaction, so find the section that matches your visit and check your paperwork before you arrive. Vehicle Purchase Please bring the following: Valid Identification A current state or government-issued photo ID. Vehicle Title or Manufacturer’s Certificate of Origin (MCO) Must be properly completed for transfer of ownership. No write-overs, cross-outs, mutilations, or use of white-out. Secure Odometer Disclosure (2011 and Newer Vehicles) Mileage must be completed by the seller. Typically included on the vehicle title. Damage Disclosure –SFN 18609 (Completed by Seller) Required for vehicles less than nine model years old (current model year plus previous eight). Seller must provide a completed SFN 18609. Bill of Sale Required for purchases from a dealership. Large trucks may require an empty weight ticket. Payment Methods Accepted Cash, Check, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express Additional Documentation Depending on your transaction, additional forms may be required. Tip: Review all forms for accuracy before your appointment to help avoid delays. Update Vehicle Information Please bring the following: Valid Identification A current state or government-issued photo ID. Vehicle Title or Manufacturer’s Certificate of Origin (MCO) Must be properly completed for transfer of ownership. No write-overs, cross-outs, mutilations, or use of white-out. Secure Odometer Disclosure (2011 and Newer Vehicles) Mileage must be completed by the seller. Typically included on the vehicle title. Damage Disclosure –SFN 18609 (Completed by Seller) Required for vehicles less than nine model years old (current model year plus previous eight). Seller must provide a completed SFN 18609. Bill of Sale Required for purchases from a dealership. Large trucks may require an empty weight ticket. Payment Methods Accepted Cash, Check, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express Additional Documentation Depending on your transaction, additional forms may be required. Tip: Review all forms for accuracy before your appointment to help avoid delays. Transfer Vehicle to North Dakota (If You Recently Moved to ND) Lien Change Bring: Valid state or government-issued photo ID Lien release (required only when removing a lien) Title (a lien release may be required if the title is lost) Vehicle information (VIN, plate number, or title number) Payment Address Change Bring: You must be listed as the vehicle owner VIN or plate number for each vehicle needing an address update Valid state or government-issued photo ID Payment (if applicable) Legal Name Change Bring: North Dakota title Current vehicle mileage Valid state or government-issued photo ID showing your new legal name Payment Add or Remove an Owner Bring: North Dakota vehicle title (if available) or required ownership documents Current vehicle mileage Valid state or government-issued photo ID Vehicle information (VIN, plate number, or title number) Payment Note: Additional documentation may be required depending on your situation. Tip: Review all forms for accuracy before your appointment to help avoid delays. Renew Registration or Register a Vehicle Bring: Renewal invoice or vehicle information (VIN, plate number, or title number) Valid state or government-issued photo ID Photo of the VIN plate (may be required) Large Truck Renewal (over 54,999 pounds): Current IRS 2290 with correct VIN and valid eFile watermark or IRS stamp Vehicle information (VIN, plate number, or title number) Inspection Documents (If Required) For vehicles previously branded “SALVAGE” bring: Salvaged Title SFN 2486 – Certificate of Motor Vehicle Inspection , or SFN 58953 – Certificate of Low-Speed, Off-Highway, and Trailer Inspection Payment Methods Accepted: Cash, Check, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express Note: Additional documentation may be required depending on your transaction. Tip: Review all forms for accuracy before your appointment to help avoid delays.
