 Chapter 7: TITLE TO VEHICLES
 Subchapter 2: CERTIFICATE OF TITLE AND CERTIFICATE OF SALVAGE
 §657 
 §656. Secretary of State to check identification number
 Upon receiving an application for a certificate of title or certificate of salvage, the Secretary of State shall check the identification number in the application against the records of certificates issued and of stolen or converted vehicles.
 [PL 1993, c. 683, Pt. A, §2 (NEW); PL 1993, c. 683, Pt. B, §5 (AFF).]
