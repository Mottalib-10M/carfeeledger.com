Affirmation for Electric Vehicle
Definition (625 ILCS 5/12-805): Beginning on January 1, 2020, the registration fee for Electric vehicles shall be equal to the fee set forth in Section 3-806 for motor vehicles of the first division, other than Autocycles, Motorcycles, Motor Driven Cycles, and Pedacycles. In addition to the registration fees, the Secretary shall assess an additional $100 per year in lieu of the payment of motor fuel taxes. $1 of the additional fees shall be deposited into the Secretary of State Special Services Fund and the remainder of the additional fees shall be deposited into the Road Fund. (Source: P.A. 101-32, eff. 6-28-19.)
Fees
Random-Number Electric Vehicle License Plates
Newly acquired vehicle/first-time issuance - $416 ($165 title fee + $251 registration fee)
Currently titled vehicle/first-time issuance - $251 ($251 registration fee)
Current plates expire within 90 days - $280 ($251 registration fee + $29 replacement fee)
Current plates do not expire within 90 days - $29 ($0 registration fee + $29 replacement fee)
Annual renewal - $251
To Apply
The following documentation must be submitted to the Secretary of State’s office along with the Affirmation for Electric Vehicle :
Application Vehicle Transaction (VSD 190) ;
Appropriate tax forms and fees payable to the Illinois Department of Revenue;
Applicable fee payable to the Secretary of State;
And if a converted vehicle, submit photographs of the front, back and side of the vehicle along with photographs of the electric motor and battery.
The request can only be processed in Springfield. Mail all required documentation and the applicable fees to:
Secretary of State
Special Plates Division
501 S. Second St., Rm. 541
Springfield, Illinois 62756
Renewal Methods
Electric Vehicle license plates can be renewed by mail or visit a Secretary of State Facility or by Online Renewal .
