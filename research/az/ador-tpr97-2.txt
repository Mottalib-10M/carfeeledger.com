Source: Arizona Department of Revenue, Transaction Privilege Tax Ruling TPR 97-2, Motor Vehicle Dealer's Documentation Fees
https://azdor.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/RULINGS_TPT_1997_tpr97-2.pdf (read 2026-10-11 in Chrome; note 5/14/2021 statute references renumbered, no substantive changes)
RULING:
When a dealer sells a motor vehicle, the dealer customarily prepares and processes the
required documents to obtain title and registration on behalf of the purchaser. The motor
vehicle dealer may charge a "documentation fee" or "doc fee" for this service.
Doc fees represent charges for services rendered in addition to selling tangible personal
property. Transaction privilege tax does not apply to doc fees if the fee is reasonable with
respect to the value of the service provided and the fee is shown separately on the sales
invoice and in the dealer's books and records.
