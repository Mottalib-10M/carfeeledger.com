186.017   Transportation Cabinet to establish fees relating to titling and registering motor vehicles by administrative regulation. The Transportation Cabinet shall promulgate administrative regulations in accordance with KRS Chapter 13A to establish f ees to be paid to the cabinet and county clerks for services provided in the process of titling and registering motor vehicles under this chapter and KRS Chapter 186A. Effective: July 15, 2026 History: Created 2026 Ky. Acts ch. 135, sec. 3, effective July 15, 2026.
