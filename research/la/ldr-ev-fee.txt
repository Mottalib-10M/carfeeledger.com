 Electric and Hybrid Vehicle Road Usage Fee
 Electric and Hybrid Vehicle Road Usage Fee Instructions
 Individuals and businesses that own or lease an electric or hybrid vehicle required to be registered in Louisiana must pay a road usage fee to the Louisiana Department of Revenue (LDR) for the 2025 tax year. The fee is $110 for electric vehicles and $60 for hybrid vehicles per calendar year. Vehicles owned or leased for less than one year are subject to a prorated fee.
 Electric Vehicles Rate of Fee
 The fee for a calendar year is $110 for electric vehicles. Vehicles owned or leased for less than one year are subject to a prorated fee as shown below.

 Date Fee Due
 Returns and payments are due on or before May 15th, 2026 .
 When can the fee be reported and paid?
 Owners/lessees may report and pay the fee:
 Before the end of the year the vehicle acquired
 Before the end of the year the vehicle was sold, transferred, or donated
 On or before May 15th, 2026 
 Purpose of the fee?
 The Road Usage Fee ensures electric and hybrid vehicle owners contribute to roadway maintenance and infrastructure, similar to fuel taxes.
 How to file
 Individual Owners/Lessees
 File Form R-19000 ,
 Submit through LaTAP , or
 Attach Form R-19000A (Page 2 of Form R-19000) to your 2025 Individual Income Tax Return IT-540 
 Businesses or other entities
 File Form R-19000 , or
 Submit through LaTAP (requires 10-digit LDR account ID)
 Additional Payment Option (OMV)
 The Road Usage Fee may also be paid through the Office of Motor Vehicles (OMV):
 👉 https://la.accessgov.com/services/Forms/Page/services/evfee/1 
 ⚠️ Important: Pay the fee only once. Do not pay through OMV and also on your tax return or LaTAP.
 Refunds – 2025 Only
 If you paid the Road Usage Fee through OMV and LDR (on your 2025 tax return or Form R-19000):
 Do not amend your return 
 Do not file Form R-20127 
 💡 Recommendation (Avoid Delays & Duplicate Payments)
 Choose one payment method only (OMV, LaTAP, or tax return)
 If you already paid through OMV, do not pay again on your tax return
 Keep your payment confirmation for your records
 Use LaTAP to report and monitor your LDR account
 Important
 Form R-19000 cannot be amended 
 Refunds apply only to payments made to LDR 
 OMV payments are not refunded by LDR 
 Future Payments (Beginning 2026)
 Road Usage Fees for 2025 and prior years must be paid to LDR 
 Beginning in 2026 , OMV will collect the fee for 2026 and forward
