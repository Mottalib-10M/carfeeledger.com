Dear Ms. Casey: 
Act 502 of 2025 directs the legislative auditor to verify the inflation 
adjustment calculated by the division of administration for the maximum fee 
amount authorized by R.S. 6:969.18(A)(2). The seller of a motor vehicle is 
authorized to charge this fee for credit investigation, compliance with federal 
and state law, preparation of the documents necessary to perfect or satisfy a 
lien upon the objects sold, and any other functions incidental to the titling of 
the retail sale. 
~ 
We received the division of administration's calculation that the adjusted 
maximum fee for calendar year 2026 is $436. We have verified that this 
amount is accurate. 
To this end, we obtained consumer price index data from the U.S. Bureau of 
Labor Statistics and verified the accuracy of the data used and the 
calculations performed by the division of administration.
