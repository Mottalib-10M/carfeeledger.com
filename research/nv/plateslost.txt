 Stolen Plates or Stolen Vehicle
 Order replacement plates with the same number and style.
 Report to Law Enforcement: It is recommended that you report
 any lost or stolen plates to your local law enforcement agency if your license
 plates are used unlawfully. The DMV does not require a police report but
 strongly recommends one for your protection.
 Vehicle Was Stolen
 Your entire vehicle has been stolen
 Steps to Take:
 File a police report with local law enforcement 
 Complete the
 Lost, Stolen or Mutilated
 License Plate Affidavit (VP 202) 
 Bring the affidavit and any police report to a DMV
 office 
 DMV will cancel the registration and mark plates as
 stolen 
 Important: This protects you from liability
 if the vehicle or plates are used in illegal activities.
 Only Plates Were Stolen
 Vehicle is safe, but plates are missing
 Steps to Take:
 File a police report recommended 
 Apply in person at a DMV office 
 You must obtain a new plate number and cannot keep the
 same number 
 Bring the
 VP 202 affidavit ,
 any remaining plate if you have one, and police report if
 available.
 New Number Required: For security reasons,
 you cannot duplicate the stolen plate number. You will
 receive a new number.
 Fees & Registration Renewal
 Replacement: All original license plate fees apply, plus a
 $5 substitute plate fee. Standard plates cost $16 total, while other styles
 vary.
 Full-Year Renewal: You may choose to renew your
 registration for a full year at this time.
 Emissions: You must complete an emission inspection if one
 is required and your last smog test was more than 90 days ago.
 Expiration Date: Your expiration date will be extended if
 your current one is more than 35 days away.
 Credit: You will receive credit for the unused portion of
 your current Nevada registration.
 Registration Options for New Plates
 When you receive your new license plates, you have two choices for handling your
 vehicle’s registration.
 Option 1: Keep Your Current Expiration Date
 Choose this if your registration is not due for renewal soon and you
 want to keep your existing schedule.
 What you get: A new registration slip and a decal
 that matches your current expiration month/year.
 Requirements: None. You do not need a new emission
 inspection or a registration renewal.
 Temporary Use: If your plates are being mailed, we
 will issue a temporary movement permit to keep you legal on the road
 in the meantime.
 Option 2: Renew for a Full Year
 Choose this if you would rather reset your registration clock now.
 The Change: Your expiration date will be extended
 by one full year. This only applies if your current expiration is
 more than 35 days away.
 Emission Inspection: A new test is required if:
 Your vehicle typically requires one.
 Your last test was completed more than 90 days ago.
 Registration Credit: You will not lose money on
 your current registration. The DMV will apply a credit for the
 unused portion of your current year toward the new fee.
