 
 Nevada DMV - License Plate Reissue
 License Plate Reissue
 Nevada law requires license plates to be replaced on a scheduled cycle. Your
 renewal notice will indicate whether new plates will be issued with your registration
 renewal.
 Important Notice: The rolling reissue program has ended as
 of October 1, 2023. See AB 457 .

 License Plate Reissue
 How It Works
 Receiving Your Plates
 If your license plates are more than eight years old, you may
 receive replacement license plates in the mail after you renew
 your registration. Please make sure we have your correct
 address.
 Separate Mailings
 Plates and decals are mailed separately. Place your new decal on
 your new plates when you receive them. Do Not 
 put the decal on your old plates.
 Lost or Misplaced Decal
 If you lose the decal or put it on the wrong plate, the fee for
 a
 Duplicate
 Decal 
 is $5.00.
 Unreadable Plates
 If your plates are unreadable and you are not due for a reissue,
 please order
 Duplicate
 License Plates .
 Renewal Notification
 Renewal Postcard Without Plate Reissue: Renewal postcards,
 Online Services ,
 and kiosks will indicate when no license plate reissue is required. In this
 case, your notice will show only standard renewal information.
 Renewal Postcard With Plate Reissue: If your renewal postcard
 shows that your plates are due for reissue, new plates will be produced and
 mailed after you complete your registration renewal.
 Notice on an Internet Registration Renewal: When renewing
 online, the system will clearly notify you if your license plates are scheduled
 for reissue. New plates will be manufactured and mailed once the renewal is
 processed.
 Fees
 Plate Cost Recovery Fee
 Renewals may or may not include a Plate Cost Recovery Fee of $3.00
 per plate depending on the style.
 Generally, this fee will be applied to plates with the standard
 Home Means Nevada background, including Classic Vehicle and
 similar plates.
 Specialty plates already include a production fee with the
 renewal and will be charged only the Prison Industry Fee.
 Prison Industry Fee
 Registration renewals that include replacement plates will include a
 Prison Industry Fee of 50 cents per plate in
 addition to all other applicable registration fees.
 Recycling Old Plates
 The DMV encourages our customers to recycle old license plates with us to ensure proper disposal unless the original owner wishes to keep them.
 How to Recycle
 You can mail them to the Carson City DMV or return them to a DMV office. Please include a note specifying the reason for the return. No appointment is necessary.

 License Plate Reissue
 Background Changes
 Plates with the Sunset background will be converted to the Home Means Nevada background.
 Specialty plates will keep the same style.
 Plate Numbers
 New plates will have the same number. Nearly all replacement plates will have embossed lettering.
 Exempt Styles
 Nearly all standard and specialty plates including
 Discontinued Plates 
 are subject to the plate reissue.
 The following plate styles are exempt:
 Blue Plates
 Manufactured through December, 1981
 Circa 1982 Replica
 Manufactured through June, 2015
 Anniversary Plates
 Nevada State 150th and 125th
 UNLV/UNR Plates :
 UNLV Plate History: The UNLV license plate has been redesigned twice since it was first issued in 1993.
 UNR Plate History: The UNR license plate has been updated once since its original release in 1993.
 No Requirement to Switch Designs: Registered owners were not required to change to the newer plate styles when redesigns were introduced.
 Reissue Uses Original Style: When your plates are reissued, you will receive the original style that was issued at the time you first purchased the plates.
 Incorrect Style Will Be Replaced: If your reissued UNLV or UNR plates arrive in the wrong style, the DMV will remake and mail the correct plate at no charge.
