 MSRP Information
 Manufacturers Suggested Retail Price - when the vehicle was NEW. (What the 
 vehicle retailed for when first offered for sale). The MSRP on new vehicles can be obtained from the Internet and other 
 printed sources. The MSRP on used vehicles can be determined from the
 vehicle registration.
 This estimate will be based on the MSRP you entered. Fees may be different depending
 on the actual MSRP.
 We will search the DMV database using the VIN you enter. If the VIN search was successful,
 we will use the MSRP from our database. Otherwise, we will use the MSRP you have entered. 
 An accurate MSRP is essential in determining your ESTIMATED registration fees.
 Nevada governmental services taxes are calculated on the depreciated MSRP. See our 
 FAQs for a sample calculation.

 Vehicle Type Information
 Auto, Passenger Van, Motor Home 
 Use this type for most private passenger cars, pickups,
 SUVs (Sport Utility Vehicles), under 6,000 pounds declared gross weight, including commercial vehicles
 which operate within Nevada only.  Use this type also for all
 private motor homes and RVs regardless of weight.
 VIN = Vehicle Identification 
 Number. - This is found on your registration slip, title, Dealers
 Report of Sale or the lower driver's side corner of the windshield on most
 vehicles. Be sure to check this number carefully and type it in accurately.
 Motorcycle 
 Motorcycles over 50 cc's and capable of speeds over 30 miles per hour must
 be registered and insured to be driven on public streets and highways.
 Motorcycle registrations are assessed a Motorcycle Safety Fee to fund
 training and safety programs from the Nevada
 Office of Traffic Safety
 .  Visit www.NevadaRider.com 
 or call (800) 889-8779.
 Learn critical skills
 needed to ride safely. Take a Motorcycle Safety Foundation sponsored Rider
 Course. Call toll free (800) 446-9227. Be sure to wear proper protective
 clothing.
 Please remember a helmet is required in Nevada. Drive Safe. Ride
 sober. Ride responsible.
 Truck, Cargo Van, Bus 
 Use this type for any vehicle, other than private motor homes, which is over
 6,000 lbs. declared gross weight. Select the proper weight range from the
 menu.  This is used in calculating the basic registration fee.
 If you operate any vehicle in interstate commerce, you
 must obtain an apportioned registration through the DMV Motor Carrier
 section.  Any vehicle which weighs 26,001 pounds or more also must
 register through Motor Carrier. Motor Homes are considered a recreational
 vehicle regardless of weight and are exempt from Motor Carrier registration
 requirements.   CLICK HERE 
 or call 1-877-368-7828 ext. 5. 
 Trailer i.e. Utility, Boat 
 Use this type for utility and boat trailers.  The unladen weight is 
 listed on your registration slip.
 The Registration Fee is based on weight for utility, boat trailers, travel trailers,
 5th wheels, and tent trailers.   Governmental Services and
 Supplemental Government Services taxes are based on value and age (depreciated MSRP).
 Travel Trailer, 5th Wheel, Tent 
 Registration Fee is $27.00 plus Governmental Services Tax and 
 Supplemental Governmental Services Tax. (see explanation in Cost Info) 
 Trailers only need one plate which costs $.50 for the Prison Industries 
 Fee.
 Moped 
 Mopeds must be registered if the motor 50cc and under, or 1500 watts and under, and the
 moped is capable of no more than 30 miles per hour. Insurance is not required.
 You must hold a Class C or higher driver’s license.
