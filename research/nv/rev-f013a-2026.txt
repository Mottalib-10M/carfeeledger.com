     SALES AND USE TAX: SUPPLEMENTAL SCHEDULE B                                                              - LIQUOR SURCHARGE FEE INSTRUCTIONS

This schedule                       is for use by a covered food establishment   (establishment) registered with the Department that sells alcoholic beverages  to consumers for
                            to-go or delivery by the establishment, or by a delivery support service acting on behalf of an establishment.

Pursuant to Nevada Revised Statutes  369.4891, an  establishment may sell at retail an alcoholic beverage in a container sealed by the     establishment for
consumption off the premises after the board of county commissioners of a county or the governing body of an incorporated city enacts an ordinance
authorizing such sales. Such alcoholic beverages may also be delivered by a delivery support service acting on behalf of an establishment.

A $.50 surcharge applies to the retail sale of each      single serving of an alcoholic beverage sold in a container sealed by a         n establishment for consumption
off the premises. A single serving of an alcoholic beverage is the volume of an individual alcoholic beverage according to the menu, recipe or practice
of the          establishment making the sale to a consumer. If multiple servings of an alcoholic beverage are sold at retail and sealed in a single container for
consumption off the premises, the surcharge must be collected for each such serving. The surcharge is to be charged, collected and remitted to the
Department by the     establishment, or by a delivery support service acting on behalf of an establishment                        , on the establishment's or delivery support
service's monthly or quarterly REV-F013A Combined Sales and Use Tax Return on Page 1, Line 23.
