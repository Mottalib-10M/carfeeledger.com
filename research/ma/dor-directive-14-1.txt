Directive 14-1 (03/19/2014), Massachusetts Department of Revenue
Directive: Separately stated document preparation fees and title preparation fees charged by a motor vehicle dealer in connection with the sale of a motor vehicle are part of the sales price subject to sales tax, regardless of whether they are paid at the time of the sale or financed.
... Where applicable, the excise is imposed at the rate of 6.25 percent of the sales price of the property or services sold. G.L. c. 64H, §§ 1, 2. A complementary 6.25 percent use tax ... G.L. c. 64I, §§ 1, 2.
