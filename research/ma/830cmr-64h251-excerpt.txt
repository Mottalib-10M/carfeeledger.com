(5)(b)2.a. Sales price. The sales price of a motor vehicle, trailer or other vehicle transferred in a casual and isolated sale is the greater of either 1) the actual amount paid by the purchaser for the vehicle, or 2) the average trade-in value of the vehicle.
(5)(c)1. Sales by Massachusetts dealers. If the sale is by a Massachusetts dealer in the regular course of business and the purchaser either previously paid a tax on the vehicle traded-in, or is exempt ..., the sales tax is computed on the sales price, reduced by any amount credited towards the sales price by reason of a trade-in.
(5)(c)2. Casual and isolated sales and transfers. If the sale or transfer is a casual and isolated sale or transfer, the use tax is computed on the sales price, which may not be reduced by any amount credited towards the sales price by reason of a trade-in.
